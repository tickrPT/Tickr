About Tickr

Tickr is a free online timer built for three things people do every day: Pomodoro focus sessions for deep work and study, classroom timers for teachers running lessons, and HIIT interval timers for workouts at home or in the gym.

Three modes, one timer

Pomo — Classic 25/5 work-break cycles, plus custom focus and break lengths.

Class — Single countdown with school-bell sound, fullscreen projector view, and quick presets for activities, group work and quizzes.

HIIT — Work/rest intervals with rounds for Tabata-style training. Set 30 seconds of exercise and 10 seconds of rest, choose your rounds, and Tickr cycles through automatically.

No signup. No tracking. No clutter.

Just open and run. Works on phone, tablet and desktop.
